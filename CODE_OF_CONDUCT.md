# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and participants respectfully regardless of identity or views. Harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory abuse and publication of personal voting choices are prohibited. Critique proposals and evidence, not personal characteristics.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict abusive participation with a recorded reason. Decisions can be appealed to the repository owner through a private GitHub contact or a non-sensitive moderation issue. Do not repeat harmful personal data in an appeal. Maintainers are subject to the same rules and must disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
